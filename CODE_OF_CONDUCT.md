# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want AverQel to be a welcoming, respectful, and technically rigorous
community for every contributor, regardless of background, identity,
experience, or role.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while addressing impact clearly.
- Welcome different technical viewpoints.
- Protect private data, credentials, and security-sensitive information.
- Focus feedback on the work, not on the person.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, doxxing,
non-consensual publication of private information, sexualized behavior, and
deliberate exposure of credentials or vulnerabilities are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Do not report security vulnerabilities in public issues. Follow
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) for security reports.

For conduct concerns, email `support@averqel.com` with the relevant links,
dates, and context. Do not include passwords, tokens, customer documents, or
exploit details. Reports will be handled confidentially and reviewed fairly.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions or
pull requests, restrict participation, or remove access when behavior violates
this policy. The response will be proportionate to the behavior and its
impact. Maintainers will communicate decisions privately when possible and
will protect the reporter's privacy.

Appeals may be sent to the same address with the decision and relevant context.
Security reports and conduct reports are handled separately; never put
security-sensitive details in a conduct report.

This policy is adapted from the principles of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
